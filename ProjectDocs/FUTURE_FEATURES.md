# Nice-To-Have Future Features

This is a future/backlog list, not current Milestone 1 scope. These ideas should be considered after the core loop is fun: choose a survivor, enter the dungeon, survive or suffer consequences, return to the village, bank resources, upgrade, and repeat.

## Character And Death

- Randomly generated recruit names.
- Renameable characters.
- Random starting stats and traits.
- Cursed aging on defeat.
- Corpse recovery missions before resurrection can begin.
- Resurrection Ward building and resurrection-state visuals.
- Fallen characters unavailable for multiple runs.
- Permanent death at age 100.
- Dead character memorial or graveyard.
- Equipment loss or equipment damage on defeat.
- Character history log: expeditions survived, defeats, years spent returning, injuries, kills, recovered corpses, and village jobs held.

## Corpse Recovery

- Defeated characters leave a body in the dungeon instead of returning automatically.
- Another character must recover the corpse before ward resurrection can begin.
- Corpse recovery appears as a special expedition objective.
- The corpse can take inventory capacity, slow movement, or increase stress while carried.
- Abandoned corpses can become corrupted, lost forever, or turn rooms more dangerous.
- The dungeon can remember death locations and make those rooms worse on later runs.

## Stress And Horror

- Stress meter per character.
- Older characters gain stress tolerance.
- Younger characters have better physical stats but stress faster.
- High stress makes the dungeon darker.
- High stress strengthens enemies.
- Full stress can trigger panic, collapse, aging, or shadow summons.
- Stress-based hallucinations.
- Stress injuries and traumas.
- Shadow enemies born from failed, abandoned, or highly stressed characters.

## Village Management

- Tavern food and drink stock.
- Beds as purchasable recovery capacity.
- Medical supplies for injury treatment.
- Resurrection supplies for fallen characters.
- Expandable tavern rooms.
- Assign characters to village jobs.
- Older or retired characters can become village workers.
- Worker characters can still be sent on runs at the risk of losing their village service.
- Losing a worker disables or weakens that village service.
- Village service upgrades: tavern, blacksmith, resurrection ward, market, scout table, medical station.

## Economy And Supplies

- Buy expedition supplies before a run.
- Torches, food, bandages, antidotes, keys, ritual charms, and corpse bags.
- Limited carried inventory.
- Split gold/relic risk between carried pouch and safe bank.
- Dungeon merchants.
- Rare crafting materials from deeper rooms.
- Supply shortages affecting recovery, morale, and resurrection.

## Expeditions

- Corpse recovery expeditions.
- Optional extraction rooms.
- Branching dungeon routes.
- Dangerous "keep going" rewards.
- Room modifiers: haunted, flooded, cursed, collapsed, infested, silent, blood-marked.
- Boss or objective rooms.
- Rescue survivors from dungeon rooms.
- Procedural quest objectives.
- Deeper routes with escalating extraction risk.

## Combat

- More enemy archetypes.
- Enemy telegraphed attacks.
- Blocking and dodging.
- Directional melee later.
- Weapon types with different reach, speed, stamina cost, and risk profile.
- Armor and armor damage.
- Character-specific combat builds.
- Injuries affecting combat behavior.

## Presentation

- Dark stylized low-poly art direction with horror lighting.
- Strong fog, darkness, silhouettes, and audio pressure.
- Better village UI panels.
- Physical station menus instead of debug HUDs.
- Character portraits.
- Death, aging, resurrection, and corpse recovery ceremony visuals.
- Reactive audio: stress whispers, heartbeat, distant dungeon sounds, ward rituals, tavern recovery ambience.

## Long-Term Big Ideas

- Co-op multiplayer spike.
- Village workers and expedition characters as the same roster.
- Corruption spreading from abandoned corpses.
- Retired veterans shaping the village economy.
- Legacy system where dead characters leave heirlooms, curses, or village scars.
- A final "too old to resurrect" ritual choice with major consequences.
